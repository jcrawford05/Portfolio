Apex Labs

A desktop simulator where you play race engineer: set up a Formula-style car within budget, mass and regulation limits, run it around a circuit, and see why the lap time changed.

  • Lap-time model that finds each corner's speed limit from downforce and tire grip, then works out acceleration and braking along the track.
  • Simulated laps are checked against historical F1 timing and telemetry from OpenF1.
  • A live web dashboard of historical race results from 1950 to 2024, with filters and CSV export.

Exploratory data analysis

Socioeconomic Factors in Automotive Accident Fatalities

How do income, education, employment and inequality relate to fatal crash rates across U.S. states? The analysis looks for patterns and treats them as correlation, not cause.

  • Merged state-level crash data with Census tables on education, inequality and employment.
  • Employment ratio had the strongest relationship with the fatal-collision rate (r = −0.59).
  • Written up as a reproducible Jupyter notebook.

Python · pandas · statsmodels · seaborn
